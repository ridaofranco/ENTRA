Guide for organizers

A dance school's end-of-year recital: tickets for the families

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 6 min read

The end-of-year recital (in Argentina the school year ends in December) is the most awaited night for a dance, drama or music school, and also the one that gives the front office the most work: not enough tickets, families who want five, money in envelopes. This guide sorts out the allocation per student, the shows and payment so all of that is settled before the date.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

Before opening sales: three numbers

An end-of-year recital has an audience you already know: your students' families. That makes promotion easier, but it makes allocation harder, because everyone wants a seat and the theater has the seats it has. Before loading anything, sit down with these three numbers:

  • Seats per show: the real capacity of the room, minus the seats lost to the sound booth, the filming camera or the front rows if groups waiting to go on use them.
  • Students per show: how many dance, act or play in each one. If a student appears in two shows, decide which one their family is counted in.
  • Tickets per student: divide the seats by the students and round down. If you get 3.4, the rule is 3 per student, and whatever is left is sold later as general admission.

If the math gives fewer than two tickets per student, no sales setup will fix it: you need another show. Open it before selling, not when there are already angry families.

One show or several

Schools with many groups often put on two or three shows the same weekend, each with a different running order. To sell them you have two paths in ENTRÁ:

  • One event per show. Each show has its own date, time, cap and link. It is the clearest for families and for the door: in access control you select that show's event and only those tickets get through. It is the option we recommend when shows are on different days or at different times.
  • A single event with one ticket type per show, for example "Saturday 6 pm" and "Sunday 11 am", each with its own cap. It works if you want a single link, but it requires the door to look at the ticket type the scanner shows so a Saturday ticket doesn't get in on Sunday.

In both cases, publish next to the link which groups perform in each show. The question the front office will get most is "which show is my daughter dancing in?", and that is answered on the event page, not over WhatsApp one by one.

How many tickets per student: what you can control

Let's be clear about this: ENTRÁ has no cap on tickets per student. Each purchase can take up to 4 tickets of each type and each type has a total cap that closes on its own when it fills up, but the same person can buy again. To make the "three per student" rule stick, there are three ways to set it up, from less to more control:

  • A written rule and a question at purchase. You add your own question to the event, for example "Student's name and group", and write the rule in the description. Since the attendee list is exported as CSV with one column per question, the front office sorts it by student in Excel and immediately sees who went over. Anyone who bought too many gets a word before the show.
  • Hidden event. You mark the event as hidden: it doesn't appear in the public listings and only people with the link can get in. The link is sent through the families' group, so the recital doesn't fill up with outsiders before the people who should buy have bought.
  • Complimentary tickets for the included seats. If the school fee already includes two tickets per student, don't sell them: issue them from the Cortesías (complimentary) tab with the family member's name, email, phone and ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID), the ticket type and the quantity. Each family gets their QR by email and the dashboard keeps a summary of what was issued. Extra tickets, if there are seats left, are sold as general admission.

And discount codes? In ENTRÁ discount codes exist, by percentage or fixed amount, with an expiry date and a usage limit. But careful: the limit counts purchases, not tickets. A single use code works for one purchase, even if that purchase takes five tickets. Use them for what they are good for: a lower price for students' families, for example, with a code that only circulates in the school group.

Price and payment without the front office losing its mind

The end-of-year chaos in the front office almost always comes from cash: envelopes, paper lists, change, the aunt who pays by bank transfer and sends the receipt at eleven at night. Moving payment to online sales puts an end to that:

  • Each family buys from their phone, without creating an account, and gets the ticket by email with its QR. Payment is processed by Mercado Pago (the Argentine payment platform).
  • You collect 100% of the price you set. The service charge and payment processing are paid by the buyer, itemized at checkout before confirming.
  • The front office stops taking notes. In the dashboard it sees tickets sold, revenue, the breakdown by type and the list of who bought, when and what.
  • If someone typed their email wrong, it is corrected from the dashboard and the ticket is resent.

About the price: add up what the recital costs (venue, sound, lights, filming if you pay for it) and divide it by the tickets you will really sell, not counting complimentary ones. If there are little siblings who will sit on a lap, decide from what age they pay and write it down; how to handle tickets for kids and adults is covered in kids' shows: tickets for children and adults.

When to open sales

For a recital, a two-step order works well:

  • First, families only. You mark a "Families" ticket type as presale, and until you tap Lanzar venta oficial (launch official sale) in the dashboard, the public sees only that one.
  • Then, whatever is left. When you launch the official sale, the presale ends and the other types open, for example a "General" for friends and neighbors with whatever seats remain.

Meanwhile, from the dashboard you can edit prices and quantities and add new ticket types at any time. If one show sells out fast, expand the other or add a third.

What to put on the event page

  • Which groups perform in each show and in roughly what order.
  • The tickets per student rule, written in one line.
  • What time the doors open and what time it ends. Families want to know if they can make it after work.
  • Whether filming or photos are allowed, or whether the school hires the filming and sells it separately.
  • Where students leave their things and which door they use, which is not the audience door.

The door on the day of the recital

At a recital the door gets complicated because everyone arrives together, ten minutes before, with flowers and grandparents. To keep it flowing:

  • One or two people scanning with a phone, no scanners needed. Each QR is valid only once: if someone forwarded the screenshot, whoever arrives first gets in.
  • Anyone who can't find the email is looked up by surname, ID number or email from the scanner, without stopping the line.
  • If the room has poor signal, the scanner warns that it is validating offline and keeps working.
  • Students don't go through that door. They come in earlier, another way, with a list from the school.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set a cap on tickets per student?

ENTRÁ has no cap per student: each purchase takes up to 4 tickets of each type and each type has a total cap. To enforce the rule, add your own question with the student's name, export the list as CSV and check who went over, or issue the included tickets as complimentary tickets from the dashboard.

Is it better to have one event per show or a single one?

If the shows are on different days or at different times, one event per show is better: each one has its own cap and link, and at the door the scanner only lets that show's tickets through.

How do I keep the recital from filling up with outsiders?

Mark the event as hidden, so it doesn't appear in the listings and only people with the link can get in, and send it through the families' group. If you want to open up what is left, use a presale for families and then launch the official sale.

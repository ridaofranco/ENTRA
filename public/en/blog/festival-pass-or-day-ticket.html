Festival pass or day ticket: how to decide

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 6 min read

At a multi-day event, the way you sell the ticket shapes everything that comes after: how much each person pays, how capacity fills up, what happens at the door and what you say in your promotion. Here are the questions that tell you which option suits you and how each one works at ENTRÁ.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

The two options, one line each

When you load a multi-day event on ENTRÁ, you choose the entry mode, and that choice applies to every ticket for the event:

  • One QR for the whole event (festival pass). Charged once. The person gets a single QR, valid for every day, and gets in with that same code once per day.
  • One QR per day (day ticket). The buyer picks which days they will attend. The ticket price is multiplied by the number of days chosen and they get one QR per day, valid only on its date.

This guide sticks to the decision itself, not to the full setup of the festival (days, schedules, complimentary tickets, door stations).

Five questions to choose

  • Does your audience come to everything, or pick? If it is a community gathering, a convention or a festival where the experience is being there all three days, the pass is the natural choice. If each day has its own lineup and its own crowd, people will want to choose.
  • How many days are there? With two or three days, plenty of people go to everything. With five or more, few attend every day, and forcing them to pay for all of it shrinks sales.
  • Do you want going to everything to be cheaper? In day ticket mode, someone who picks every day pays the price of each one, with no automatic discount. If your strategy is to reward people who come to everything with a lower price, that is a pass.
  • Does any day have a different limit? Capacity is not set per day (more on that below). If one day the venue is smaller or the permit is different, that outweighs every other question.
  • How simple does the door need to be? With a pass, one QR per person for the whole event. With day tickets, each person has one QR per day and has to show that day's code at the door.

What changes in the price

With a pass, the price you set on each ticket type is the price of the whole event. It is charged once, however many days there are.

With a day ticket, the price you set is for one day. If someone picks two, they pay double; if they pick all of them, they pay for all of them. What this mode does not do is lower the price on its own when every day is chosen. If you want to give a benefit to people who come more days, the tool you have is the discount codes in the dashboard, by percentage or fixed amount, with a usage limit.

How to think about the price of each type and your tiers is covered in how to price your tickets and set up early bird tiers.

What changes in capacity

This is the point people miss most. At ENTRÁ capacity is set per ticket type for the whole event, and each ticket bought takes one spot, however many days it covers. A three-day purchase and a single-day purchase take the same: one spot.

With a pass, that is exactly what you want: each spot is one person who can come every day. With day tickets it has a consequence: if sales cluster on Saturday, capacity can sell out even though Friday still has room at the venue.

If you need an exact limit per day, the clean way is to load each day as a separate event, each with its own capacity.

What changes at the door

  • With a pass, the scanner lets the QR through once per day. If someone tries to enter twice on the same day, it shows "already entered today".
  • With a day ticket, each QR is only valid on its date. If someone shows another day's code, the scanner flags it in red as "not valid today" and says which days it does work for. It is worth reminding people in your welcome email or on social media to have that day's QR ready.
  • The automatic reminder goes out only once, the day before the first day. If you sell by day and some people only come to the last one, send them a message yourself closer to their date.

How the door works day by day is in how access control works on the day of the event.

What if I want both?

Within the same event, you can't: there is only one mode. And beware the temptation to solve it with ticket types: in pass mode, a type called "Saturday only" still unlocks every day, because the day is defined by the mode, not by the name.

You have two real options:

  • Day tickets for everything. Whoever wants to go to everything picks every day. It is the simplest to run, although it gives no cheaper pass.
  • Separate events. One event with the pass and one per day, each with its own link, capacity and list. At the door, each phone works with the event it has selected, and if it is shown a QR from another event it rejects it as "belongs to another event". It works, but ask for separate lines or make it clear at each station which event it validates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell passes and day tickets for the same event?

Not within the same event: the entry mode is chosen once and applies to every ticket. In day ticket mode, whoever wants to go to everything picks every day and pays the price of each one. If you need a cheaper pass plus single day tickets, you set it up with separate events.

How is a day ticket charged if someone picks several days?

The ticket type price is multiplied by the number of days chosen: two days, double the price. The buyer gets one QR per day, and each one is only valid on its date.

What happens if someone shows their ticket on a day they did not buy?

The scanner flags it in red as "not valid today" and shows which days that ticket is valid for. With a pass, on the other hand, the same QR gets in every day, once per day: if someone tries to enter twice on the same day, it shows they already entered today.

Is capacity counted per day or per ticket?

Per ticket, for the whole event. A three-day purchase takes one spot from its type's capacity, the same as a single-day purchase. If you need an exact limit per day, load each day as a separate event.

Your festival, sold the way it needs

With ENTRÁ you choose a full pass or a day ticket, the price is calculated from the days chosen and the scanner knows which day each QR is valid for. Creating the account and loading the event costs nothing.

Guide for organizers

Anime or cosplay convention: passes, minors, contest and queues

By SOMOS DER · October 31, 2026 · 7 min read

An anime, comics or cosplay convention brings together things that other events keep apart: people who come for one day and fans who never miss one, families with kids, a contest with its own registration, dozens of exhibitors setting up stands early and a queue that starts before the doors open. This guide sorts out how to load all of that so sales and entry don't get tangled.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

One day, two days or a whole weekend

The first thing is deciding how people get in. In ENTRÁ, when you create the event you can turn on the multi day option and load each day with its date and time. Then you choose the entry mode, which is one for the whole event:

  • One QR for the whole event. The person gets in every day with the same code, once per day. That is the pass.
  • One QR per day. The buyer picks which days they will attend and gets a code for each one. The ticket price is multiplied by the days chosen: two days, double.

In both cases, if someone shows the ticket on a day it does not cover, the scanner shows red with "no válido hoy" (not valid today) and says which days it is valid. If they try to get in twice on the same day, it shows they already entered.

What ENTRÁ does not do is set a special price for taking every day inside the per day mode. If you want a pass cheaper than the sum of the single days, there is a tidy way: load two events. One with the pass, with one QR for the whole event, and another with the day ticket, with one QR per day. Split the capacity between the two before publishing. Which one suits your audience is covered in festival pass or day ticket.

Capacity: the busiest day rules

Capacity is set per ticket type for the whole event, not per day. At a two day convention Saturday usually fills up much more than Sunday, and the limit of people inside is Saturday's. Before loading the numbers:

  • Ask the venue for its licensed capacity and subtract exhibitors, staff, guests and press, who also take up space in the hall.
  • If you sell passes, each pass counts on every day. A thousand passes are a thousand people on Saturday and a thousand on Sunday.
  • If you need an exact cap per day, for example because the Saturday hall is smaller, load each day as a separate event.

Kids, teenagers and adults

Lots of families go to conventions, and the age rule should be written down before you sell, not made up at the door. Each ticket type has its own name, price and capacity, so you can set up:

  • "General" for adults and teenagers.
  • "Menor de 12" (under 12), at a reduced price, if you want to charge kids differently.
  • A rule for the youngest in the first line of the description, for example "Children under 5 get in free with an adult who has a ticket".

Also say from what age a teenager can come in alone and which ID is required. To keep a record, when you create the event you can add questions for the buyer and mark them as required. Keep in mind they are answered once per purchase. The ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID) entered at purchase belongs to the buyer: it helps find the ticket at the door, not to know the age of whoever uses it. How this is handled at a kids' event is in kids' show: tickets for children and adults.

The presale for the community

Conventions have a loyal audience that follows the event's account all year. In ENTRÁ you can mark ticket types as presale: until you press "Lanzar venta oficial" (launch official sale) in the dashboard, the public only sees those. When you launch it, the presale ends and the rest open up. A presale with a cheaper pass gives you early cash to pay for the venue and tells you how many people to expect.

For cosplay groups or fan clubs that come together, a discount code per group, by percentage or fixed amount and with a usage cap, lets you reward them and see how many times each code was used.

With ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set for each ticket. Service and processing fees are paid by the buyer, with the breakdown visible before paying.

The cosplay contest, with separate registration

The contest has its own capacity, set not by the hall but by stage time: if each contestant gets two minutes and the block lasts an hour, you can't fit more than twenty or twenty five, counting changeovers. That is why the registration should be its own event in ENTRÁ, and not a ticket type inside the convention. The reason is practical: any convention ticket type opens the main door, and a contest registration shouldn't.

When you create the contest event, add required questions. They can be text, number, a list of options or long text:

  • Character and series, as text.
  • Category, as a list of options, if there are solo, group or kids categories.
  • Whether they use music or a backing track, and how they will send it.
  • "I read the rules", as a list with a single "Yes" option.

The answers come out in the export, and with that you build the running order. In the description make it clear that contest registration does not include entry to the convention. If it is free, you can mark the event as free: people reserve their spot without paying and still get their QR, which is scanned at the contestant check in, not at the main door. How to set up a registration with limited spots, step by step, is in workshop registration with limited spots.

Exhibitors, artists and guests

Shops, illustrators in the artist alley, guest voice actors and press don't buy tickets, but they take up space. If that list lives on a sheet of paper at the door, it eats into your capacity without you noticing. From the complimentary tickets tab you load each person's name, email, phone and ID number, the ticket type, the quantity and a reason, for example the stand name. They get their QR by email. Complimentary tickets do not come out of the sales capacity, but they do count toward the total capacity shown in the dashboard. How many to give and how to control them is in how many complimentary tickets to give.

Stand rental is not charged through ENTRÁ. You settle it with each exhibitor separately, with their invoice, and ENTRÁ only handles access. Exhibitors come in early to set up: give them a time slot or a separate door.

The morning queue

The queue forms an hour before opening and almost everyone wants in at the same time. What helps most:

  • Several phones scanning. You don't need to buy scanners: each staff member scans from their phone's browser and sees in green the name and ticket type, or in red the reason. Each entry is signed with the station that validated it. How many people to put on it is in how many door staff for an event.
  • Two separate lines: one for people who already have a ticket and another for those who haven't bought yet. ENTRÁ has no box office and does not record cash sales: whoever arrives without a ticket buys from their phone, without creating an account, using a sign with the QR of the event link.
  • The QR open before reaching the front. If someone can't find the email, you look them up by name, email or ID number.
  • No signal does not stop the door. If the internet goes down in the hall, entries are stored on the phone and synced when it comes back.

The QR is valid once per day. If the convention lets people go out to eat and come back in, hand out a day wristband or stamp at the same scan, and check re entry with that.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell a pass and a day ticket in the same event?

The entry mode is chosen once per event: one QR for the whole event, valid once per day, or one QR per day, where the buyer picks the days and pays for each one. If you want both at different prices, load the pass as one event and the day ticket as another, and split the capacity between them.

How do I charge for cosplay contest registration?

As a separate event, with its own capacity and required questions (character, series, category). If you load it as a ticket type inside the convention, that QR also opens the main door. Make it clear in the description that contest registration does not include the ticket.

How do exhibitors and guests get in?

With complimentary tickets: you load each person's name, email, phone and ID number, the ticket type, the quantity and a reason, for example the stand name. They get the QR by email. They do not come out of the sales capacity but they do count toward the total capacity. Stand rental is charged outside ENTRÁ.

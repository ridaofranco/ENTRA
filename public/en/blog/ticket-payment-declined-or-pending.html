Guide for buyers

Ticket payment declined or pending: what to do

By SOMOS DER · November 27, 2026 · 6 min read

If, when you come back from Mercado Pago, the screen says Pago en proceso (payment in progress), you do not need to do anything: once the payment is approved, your ticket arrives by email on its own. If it says El pago no se completó (the payment was not completed), nothing was charged and you can try again, with another payment method if needed. The one thing to avoid is paying again while the first payment is still in progress. Here is each case, step by step.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS), and payments go through Mercado Pago. This guide describes what happens on ENTRÁ when a payment is pending or declined.

First: what the screen says when you come back

When you finish paying, Mercado Pago sends you back to ENTRÁ and the screen shows one of three results, always with an order number underneath (write it down or take a screenshot, it helps if you need to contact us later):

  • Pago confirmado (payment confirmed). Your tickets are being generated. They arrive by email and stay in your profile; it can take a few seconds.
  • Pago en proceso (payment in progress). Mercado Pago is still processing the payment. Once it is approved, we email you the tickets automatically.
  • El pago no se completó (payment not completed). Nothing was charged. You can try the purchase again whenever you want.

If the payment is in progress

A payment in progress is not a declined payment. It is a payment that Mercado Pago has not finished reviewing or crediting yet. It happens, for example, when the payment goes under review or when the method you chose takes time to clear.

What you need to know:

  • The ticket is only issued once the payment is approved. On ENTRÁ no QR code is issued before that, and your spot is taken off the available stock at that same moment. Until then, the ticket is not yours yet.
  • You do not need to tell anyone. When Mercado Pago approves the payment, the email with the QR code goes out on its own and the ticket shows up in Mis Tickets (my tickets) in your profile.
  • If Mercado Pago is slow to notify, the system asks it again every few minutes whether that purchase was paid. If it was approved, the ticket is issued anyway.
  • Do not pay again just in case. If the first payment is approved later, you will end up with two purchases.

If your payment was declined

When Mercado Pago declines a payment for good, besides the screen you get an email from ENTRÁ with the reason, if Mercado Pago reports it, what to do and a button to go back to the event page. It is sent only once per purchase. The most common reasons and what to do about each one:

  • Your bank needs you to authorize the purchase. Call the number on the back of the card and ask them to authorize it. Some banks let you do it from their app, in the purchases or security section. Then try again.
  • The security system did not approve the transaction. Do not retry with the same card, it will bounce again. Try another card or money in your Mercado Pago account. If you have an account, paying while logged in usually works better.
  • The card did not have enough balance or credit limit. Check what is available or pay with another method.
  • The card is not enabled for online purchases. Ask your bank to enable internet purchases, or use another card in the meantime.
  • The card number was entered wrong. Try again with the number exactly as printed, with no spaces.

One detail: before you pay, the checkout shows the total with the service and processing fees broken down. If you retry, check that the total is the one you expected.

How not to end up paying twice

The most common problem is not the decline, it is anxiety. The screen takes a while, the phone loses signal, you close the tab and buy again. Before retrying:

  • Look for the confirmation email by the event name or the word ENTRÁ. Check Spam and, if you use Gmail, the Promotions tab.
  • Open Mis Tickets (my tickets) in your profile. If the ticket is there, you already have it, even if the email did not arrive.
  • Check the Mercado Pago app or your card statement to see whether the payment shows as approved, in progress or declined.

If the payment shows as approved but you cannot find the ticket anywhere, do not buy another one: message us on WhatsApp with your name, your ID number and the order number. The same goes if you typed your email wrong when buying: we fix that by hand.

If you paid twice anyway

It happens. If you see two approved purchases for the same ticket, message us on WhatsApp with your name, your ID number and both order numbers, and we will look into it. In the meantime, do not use or transfer the extra ticket: that makes it simpler to sort out.

If you are the organizer

Your buyers will write to you when something goes wrong with a payment. Three things that help:

  • Send them this guide when they ask about a declined card.
  • If someone says they paid and got nothing, ask for the order number before the screenshot of their statement.
  • Remember that on ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set: the service and processing fees are paid by the buyer, with the breakdown in plain sight.

Frequently asked questions

My payment is in progress. Do I have the ticket?

Not yet. On ENTRÁ the ticket is only issued once Mercado Pago approves the payment, and only then is your spot taken. When the payment clears, the email with the QR code goes out on its own and the ticket appears in Mis Tickets (my tickets), without you having to do anything.

My payment was declined. Was I charged anyway?

If the screen says the payment was not completed, nothing was charged and you can try again. You also get an email with the reason for the decline when Mercado Pago reports it, what to do in each case and a button to go back to the event page.

Can I pay again while the first payment is still in progress?

It is better to wait. A payment in progress can be approved later and, if you paid again, you will end up with two purchases. Before retrying, check your email and Mis Tickets (my tickets) in your profile.

I paid twice for the same ticket. What do I do?

Message us on WhatsApp with your name, your ID number and the order numbers shown on the confirmation screen. We will look into it and tell you how to proceed. In the meantime, do not use or transfer the extra ticket.

Selling tickets for an event in Argentina?

With ENTRÁ the ticket is only issued with an approved payment, buyers get a clear notice if their card is declined and you see sales in real time from your dashboard.

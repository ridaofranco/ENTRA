Guide for organizers

After work event with tickets: price, drinks, timing and capacity

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 5 min read

An after work event (in Argentina, an "after office") competes with something very concrete: the tiredness of the workday and the urge to go home. People go if it is close, if it starts when they get off work, if they know how much they will spend and if their coworkers are going. This guide sets up the sale around those four things: price with or without drinks, timing, presales between coworkers and the bar's capacity.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

The price: with drinks or without

The first decision is what the ticket includes. Both options work, and you can offer both:

  • Ticket with drinks. Includes one or more drinks, or something to eat. The buyer knows what they will spend and the bar knows how much it will serve. It is usually the easiest to sell for an after work event.
  • Entry only. Cheaper, and everyone orders what they want at the bar. It works if the draw is the DJ, the live music or the venue.
  • Both, as different ticket types. At ENTRÁ each ticket type has its own price and capacity. "Entry + 2 drinks" and "Entry only" need no explanation.

In the event description, say exactly what the drinks include: which drinks, until what time they can be redeemed and whether there is an alcohol-free option. With ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set for each ticket; the service and processing charges are paid by the buyer, itemized at checkout. Whatever you agree with the bar about the drinks is settled between you; put it in writing before you start selling.

Timing: start when people get off work

An after work event that starts late loses everyone who starts early the next day. Things to think about:

  • Start close to quitting time for the offices in the area. If people have to kill time, they go home.
  • Set an end time. Knowing it ends at a reasonable hour makes more people say yes. At ENTRÁ you can load the event's end time, and sales stop there.
  • Choose the day carefully. Midweek days usually have less competition than Friday, but that depends on your audience: ask the people you want to bring.
  • Location is part of the timing. A bar a few blocks from the offices and close to public transport is worth more than a nice one far away.

Presales between coworkers

People go to an after work event in groups: if the team goes, they go. That is why presales work best from coworker to coworker:

  • One person to rally each group. In each company or team, someone who shares the link in the internal chat and gets the group together.
  • One code per company or team. From the event dashboard you create discount codes with a usage limit. The codes table shows how many times each one was used, so you know which group showed up. How to use them without giving the sale away is in discount codes for an event.
  • A cutoff date for the presale. A lower price until a specific day makes the group organize early instead of "we'll see on the day".
  • Hassle-free purchase. Buyers do not need to create an account, and the ticket arrives by email with its QR. Each person buys their own, or one person buys for several.

The bar's capacity

An after work event almost always happens at a bar, and the bar has its limit. Talk about it before you publish:

  • How many people fit? Ask for the venue's licensed capacity: that is your sales ceiling.
  • Is the venue exclusive or shared? If the bar stays open to the public meanwhile, your event's capacity is only part of the total. Agree on how much.
  • How many drinks can the bar serve in time? If everyone arrives at the same time with an included drink, the bar gets swamped. Cap the ticket with drinks at a number the bar can handle.

With capacity loaded on each ticket type, the dashboard shows sales in real time and, when a type runs out, it shows as sold out. What to agree with a bar when you organize there is in selling tickets for a gig at a bar.

The door

At an after work event, lots of people arrive in the same half hour, straight from work. Each ticket has a unique QR that is valid only once and is scanned from a phone, and the scanner shows the ticket type. So whoever is at the door knows right away who bought with drinks and hands over what you agreed with the bar: a wristband, a token or a stamp. If someone forgot their QR, they can be looked up by name, email or ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID).

Frequently asked questions

Should an after work event ticket include drinks?

It usually pays off, because people know in advance how much they will spend and the bar knows how much it will serve. You can offer both options as different ticket types, one with drinks and one entry only, each with its own price and capacity.

How do I run a presale for the employees of one company?

Create a discount code from the event dashboard, with a usage limit, and give it to one person on that team to share. The codes table shows how many times it was used.

How are the drinks included in the ticket handed out?

The ENTRÁ scanner shows the ticket type of each QR, so the door knows who bought with drinks. What gets handed out afterwards (wristband, token or stamp) is something you and the bar settle before the event.

Gala dinner or party with tables: how to sell the whole table

By SOMOS DER · October 31, 2026 · 7 min read

A gala dinner, a charity party or a club anniversary is sold by the table, not by the person. The buyer is usually a company, a family or a group of friends who want to sit together, and tables seat 8 or 10. This guide explains how to sell that with ENTRÁ, which has a limit of 4 tickets per type in each purchase, and how to arrange the tables without losing track of who gets in.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

First, the hall set up

At an event with tables, capacity is defined by the layout, not by the square meters. Ask the hall for the floor plan with the tables in place and count:

  • How many tables fit with aisles for the waiters and room for the stage, the dance floor or the speech platform.
  • How many seats each table has: round tables usually seat 8 or 10.
  • How many tables are committed before selling: the organizing committee's, the sponsors', the press table, the officials' table. Those don't go on sale.

With those numbers you know how many tables and how many individual seats you can offer. If you have never done the math, the guide on licensed capacity and how many tickets to sell helps you stay within the venue's permit.

The limit of 4 per purchase and how to sell the whole table

At ENTRÁ each purchase allows up to 4 tickets of each type. That clashes with a table of 10. There are three ways to solve it, and you can combine them.

1. The table as a single ticket

Create a ticket type named exactly what it is: "Table for 10 people" or "Table for 8 people", with the price of the full table and a capacity equal to the number of tables on sale. If you have 20 tables to sell, the capacity is 20.

  • One purchase, one QR, one table. The buyer pays for the whole table at once and gets one QR. At the door it is scanned once and the scanner shows the "Table of 10" type with the buyer's name.
  • The rest of the group gets in with a list. Ask the buyer for the names of the other nine in good time, and put together a printed or on-phone list per table. When the person in charge arrives and is scanned, the door lets in the others on their list. If they arrive separately, the person at the door looks up the name on that list.
  • Spell it out in the type's description: "One ticket valid for 10 people. The holder sends the guest list by the Wednesday before".

This option is the tidiest for companies and groups that already know who is coming. The downside is that the QR does not identify each person: the detailed check is done with the list.

2. Individual tickets and several purchases

If you prefer each guest to have their own QR, sell the ticket per person ("Adult place") and let the group buy in batches: 10 people need three purchases of 4, 4 and 2. It works when everyone pays their own share, which is common at a class reunion or a party among friends. Explain it on the page so nobody thinks the system failed when it won't go past 4.

3. Complimentary tickets for invited tables

Sponsor, committee or officials' tables are not sold. At ENTRÁ complimentary tickets do not take from the sales capacity, so you can issue a complimentary "Table of 10" ticket for each one and send it to the person in charge. Careful: they do count toward the hall's total capacity shown in the dashboard. How to ration them is in complimentary tickets and invitations: how many to give.

Individual seats and shared tables

For couples or people on their own without a table, create a separate type, "Seat at a shared table", with a capacity that is a multiple of the table size: three tables of 10 set aside is a capacity of 30. Make it clear that mixed tables are arranged by the organizers.

Arranging the tables with your own question

ENTRÁ has no table map or numbered seats, so you build the floor plan yourself. What you can do is add extra questions when creating the event and mark them as required. They are answered once per purchase, not per ticket. Some useful ones:

  • "Table, company or group name": lets you group purchases that need to sit close together.
  • "Who would you like to share a table with?" for those buying individual tickets in several batches.
  • "Does anyone in your group have a dietary restriction?" Vegetarians, celiacs and allergies go to the kitchen.

The answers appear in the attendee list and in the sales export. With that spreadsheet you build the floor plan, number the tables and, a week before, send each person in charge their table number by email or WhatsApp.

Price, tiers and closing date

At a dinner the big cost is the meal per person, and the hall usually asks for the final number of diners a few days before. That is why:

  • Calculate the table price from the per person cost the hall charges you, plus drinks, music, decoration and whatever you want to raise if it is a charity event.
  • Use tiers: a cheaper first tier pushes companies to confirm early. How to stagger them is in pricing tickets in tiers.
  • Close sales before the event. Load the end of sales date for the day the hall asks you for the final number. After that date you can't add diners without talking to the kitchen.

With ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set. The service and processing charges are paid by the buyer, itemized before they pay.

If it is a charity party

At a benefit dinner the money raised has a purpose and people want to know what it is. Put it in the first line of the description, with the name of the institution. If there is also an auction, a raffle or a contribution voucher, that is collected outside the ticket: ENTRÁ sells tickets at a fixed price per type and has no pay-what-you-want or donations. The night of the event

  • Reception with two lists: the scanner for the QR codes and the table list for companions. One person scans and another seats people.
  • One QR per event. If the dinner is a single night, the QR is valid once and the scanner rejects tickets from other events, so nobody sneaks in with last year's ticket.
  • If the signal drops, check-ins are saved on the phone and synced later. More details in access control on the day of the event.

If you have to call it off, cancelling the event from the dashboard emails every buyer; if you change the date, no automatic email goes out. Either way, write to each buyer yourself with the details: at a dinner it is almost always the person in charge of the table.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell a table of 10 in a single purchase?

Yes, if you load it as its own ticket type, for example "Table of 10", with the price of the full table. That ticket generates a single QR valid for the whole group. At the door it is scanned once and checked against the list of names the table buyer sent you.

Why can't I buy 10 individual tickets together?

Because ENTRÁ allows up to 4 tickets per type in each purchase. Ten people with individual tickets need three purchases, or it is better to set up a "Table of 10" type that is sold as a single ticket.

How do I know who sits with whom?

Add an extra question at checkout, for example "Who would you like to share a table with?" or "Table or company name". It is answered once per purchase and appears in the attendee list and the export, so you build the floor plan yourself.

Are sponsor or guest tables loaded as complimentary tickets?

Yes. Complimentary tickets do not take from the sales capacity, so you can issue a complimentary "Table of 10" ticket for each invited table. Remember that they do add to the hall's total capacity shown in the dashboard.

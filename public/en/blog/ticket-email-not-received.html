Guide for buyers

You paid and your ticket never reached your email: what to do

By SOMOS DER · July 29, 2026 · 6 min read

It is one of the most uncomfortable moments there is: the money is gone and nothing arrived. The good news is that in the vast majority of cases the ticket exists, and the problem is where you are looking. These are the steps, in order, from most likely to least likely.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). This guide describes how buying a ticket on ENTRÁ works.

1. Wait a few minutes and check the payment status

The ticket is issued when the payment is confirmed, not when you press the button. With a card it is usually a matter of seconds; with cash payments or transactions held for review, it can take longer to clear. If the screen told you the payment was pending, the ticket is issued automatically as soon as it is approved.

Don't buy again while you wait. If both payments are approved, you end up with two orders and one has to be refunded, which is much slower than waiting ten minutes.

2. Search for the email properly, don't just look at your inbox

This step solves most cases. The email with your ticket has a specific subject line: "Tu entrada para [event name]" (Spanish for "Your ticket for") followed by ENTRÁ. It has the QR code in the body of the message plus an attached PDF with all the tickets from the purchase.

Do this instead of scrolling:

  • Use your email's search box with the event name, or with the word ENTRÁ.
  • Check Spam / Junk and, if you use Gmail, the Promotions and Social tabs.
  • Search from a computer if you have only been looking on your phone: some email apps hide folders.
  • If it is a work email, the company filter may have blocked it. Try a personal inbox for the next step.

3. Go to your profile: the ticket is there

This is the shortcut almost nobody knows, and it solves the problem without writing to anyone. Your ticket doesn't live in the email: the email is a copy. The original is in your account.

Go to your profile on the website and open Mis Tickets (my tickets). There you have the QR code to show at the door, the code and the button to download it.

And here is the important part: even if you bought as a guest, without creating an account, the ticket still shows up. In ENTRÁ tickets are looked up by email address, not by account. You create the account (or log in) with exactly the same email you entered when buying and they appear on their own, with no paperwork and no waiting for someone to assign them to you.

4. If you reserved a spot at a free event

For free reservations the QR code appears on screen as soon as you confirm, with a button to download the tickets right there. The email is sent too, but you don't depend on it: if you already closed that screen, the reservation is still in your profile.

5. If you typed your email wrong

It is the number one cause of tickets that "don't arrive": an extra letter, a missing dot, a @gmial. If that was it, the ticket was issued and sent, but to an inbox that isn't yours, so you won't find it by searching or in your profile.

It is fixed by hand and quickly, but you have to let us know. Gather this before writing:

  • Your full name and your ID number (DNI), exactly as you entered them at purchase.
  • The event and the date.
  • The order number, which appears on the confirmation screen as "Orden #XXXXXXXX".
  • The payment receipt (a Mercado Pago screenshot works).
  • The correct email you want it sent to.

With that information the purchase is identified and the ticket is resent. The sooner you say so, the better: sorting it out on a Tuesday is trivial, sorting it out in the line at the door is not.

6. Write to us, and what to say

If you got this far and it still doesn't show up, message us on WhatsApp at +54 11 7154-0675 or write to soporte@entratickets.com with the details from the list above.

Two things help get it solved in one go: the system keeps a record of whether the email was sent or the delivery failed, and when a delivery fails an automatic alert goes to the team, precisely because someone paid and was left without their ticket. So you are not "reporting something new": you are giving the details needed to find your order.

What you shouldn't do

  • Buy again "just in case". It creates a second order and a refund.
  • Accept a QR code from a stranger offering to "fix it for you". That is exactly the moment scams show up.
  • Leave it for the day of the event. Everything that takes two minutes by message becomes impossible at the door.
  • Forward a screenshot of your QR code to a group asking for help. That code is your access: whoever scans it first gets in.

Frequently asked questions

I bought a ticket and the email never came. What do I do?

First wait a few minutes and search Spam and the Promotions tab, using the event name or the word ENTRÁ, not just looking at your inbox. If it doesn't show up, go to your profile on the website with the same email you used to buy: the ticket is there even if you bought as a guest. If it isn't there either, write to support with your name, your ID number (DNI) and the order number.

I bought as a guest, without creating an account. How do I get my ticket back?

Create an account or log in with exactly the same email you entered when buying. In ENTRÁ tickets are looked up by email address, not by account, so when you log in with that email they appear in the Mis Tickets (my tickets) section, with the QR code ready to show at the door and to download. No paperwork needed and no waiting for someone to assign them to you.

I entered the wrong email when buying the ticket. Do I lose it?

No, but it has to be fixed by hand and it is best to do it before the day of the event. The ticket was issued to the wrong email, so it won't appear in your inbox or in your profile. Write to support on WhatsApp or by email with your full name, your ID number (DNI), the event and the order number shown on the confirmation screen, and from there the recipient is corrected and the ticket resent.

I paid with Mercado Pago and the payment shows as pending. Do I already have the ticket?

Not yet. When the payment is pending, the ticket is issued only once the payment is approved, and at that moment the email goes out automatically. This is common with cash payments or with some cards held for review. Don't buy again in the meantime: if the payment is approved twice, you end up with two orders and one has to be refunded.

Still can't find it?

Write to soporte@entratickets.com or message us on WhatsApp at +54 11 7154-0675 with your name, your ID number (DNI) and the order number, and we will sort it out. There are people on the other side, not a bot.

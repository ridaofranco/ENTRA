Boat party or catamaran trip: capacity, boarding and weather

By SOMOS DER · October 31, 2026 · 7 min read

A boat party, a catamaran trip or a launch outing is sold like any other event, with three differences: the venue has a passenger limit that doesn't stretch, the departure has a time that waits for nobody and the weather can keep the boat moored. This is what to sort out before publishing the link.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

The vessel sets the capacity

On a boat, the maximum number of people on board is stated in the vessel's license, and that number includes everyone who boards. Ask whoever rents you the boat for it in writing and do the math:

  • Licensed capacity, the number on the paper.
  • Minus the crew, if the licensed number includes them.
  • Minus your team on board: DJ, bar, photographer, the person greeting at the dock if they also board.
  • Minus your guests. At ENTRÁ complimentary tickets don't come out of the sales quota, but they do count toward the total capacity shown in the dashboard. If eighty people fit on board and you will give out six invitations, sell seventy four.

At ENTRÁ each ticket type has its own quota and doesn't share it: the sum of all of them can't exceed what you have left. More in licensed capacity: how many tickets to sell.

One sailing per event or one sailing per ticket type

ENTRÁ doesn't have time slots as a separate feature. What it has are events with a date and ticket types with name, price and quota. With that you can set up the two typical situations:

  • A single sailing, like a night party that casts off at 11 pm and returns at 3 am: one event, with the types you need ("General", "Deck table", "Presale"), each with its quota.
  • Several sailings on the same day, like one hour trips at 4 pm, 5:30 pm and 7 pm: one event for that day and one ticket type per sailing, with the time at the start of the name: "Sailing 16:00", "Sailing 17:30". Each type's quota is the vessel's capacity.
  • Sailings on different days: one event per day. That way each list stays separate and the reminder ENTRÁ sends only the day before reaches each person on their date.

If you want different prices on the same sailing (adult and child, for example), you multiply types: "17:30 · Adult" and "17:30 · Child". Since quotas aren't shared, split that sailing's places between the two. The full setup is in guided tours with capacity per time slot, and it applies the same way to a boat.

If one event spans several days, the way people get in (one QR for the whole event or one per day) is chosen once when you create it. If each night is independent, one event per night is better.

Boarding has a time and the boat doesn't wait

At a venue, latecomers get in anyway. At the dock, whoever arrives after the boat has cast off stays ashore. That rule has to be written down before the person pays. In the event description include:

  • Boarding time and departure time, separately. For example: boarding from 10:15 to 10:45 pm, departure at 11:00 pm sharp.
  • What happens if you are late, plainly: "If you arrive after departure, you miss the sailing and there is no refund."
  • The exact dock: name of the river terminal or yacht club, berth number if there is one, where to enter and where to park.
  • The return time and which dock it returns to, if it is not the same one.
  • What to bring: a jacket even in summer and whether you can board with your own drinks.

The event page sells until the end time you entered, and if you didn't enter one, until three hours after the start. On a boat you don't want someone buying when the launch is already on the water: from the event edit screen you can switch it to Pausado (paused) a little before departure and purchases stop. ENTRÁ has no box office: anyone arriving without a ticket buys from their phone while sales are still open.

The door is on the dock

Checking happens before boarding, not on board. One person stationed at the foot of the gangway, with the scanner open in the phone's browser:

  • Each QR is valid only once. Green with the name and ticket type, red with the reason. If someone sent the screenshot to a friend, whoever arrives first boards.
  • Check the type shown on screen. If it says "Sailing 19:00" and the 17:30 one is boarding, they wait: the scanner doesn't reject it on its own.
  • If someone can't find the email, you look them up by surname, ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID) or email. And if there is no signal at the dock, entries are saved on the phone and the scanner keeps validating.
  • Count before casting off. The screen shows how many checked in; add your team and the crew.

If you are asked for the list of people boarding, export the attendee list from the dashboard: it includes name, email, DNI, phone, ticket type and whether they checked in. Careful: the DNI is the buyer's. If one person buys four tickets, that is four QR codes but only one DNI. If you need each passenger's name and ID, add a required long text question to the event at checkout: "Name and DNI of each person traveling."

Weather: decide the rule before selling

Wind or storms can keep the boat moored, and whoever operates the vessel decides whether to sail. Your part is the rule for the public, written before selling:

  • When you notify people. For example: "If it is called off due to weather, we will let you know on WhatsApp up to two hours before."
  • What options people have: move to another date, get a refund, or choose between the two.

How each thing is done at ENTRÁ:

  • If you reschedule, you edit the event date and tickets remain valid for the new one. The change doesn't send a notice on its own: you write to people using the email and phone from the list.
  • If you refund, you issue the refund from Mercado Pago (the Argentine payment platform). Then, in the dashboard, you mark each ticket as refunded: the place goes back into the quota and an email is prepared to let that person know the refund is in process.
  • If someone can't make the new date and you have transfers enabled, they can pass the ticket to someone else from their profile: a new QR is generated and the old one stops working.

Minors on board

Decide it before loading the event:

  • If it is a family trip, separate "Adult" and "Child" as ticket types and write from what age people pay and whether children always travel with a responsible adult. A baby on a lap is still a person on board and counts toward capacity, even if they don't pay. More detail in kids' shows: child and adult tickets.
  • If it is a party with a bar, the age rule for alcohol is not yours to set: Argentina's Law 24.788 (in Spanish) says in its article 1 that the sale of any kind of alcoholic beverage to people under eighteen is prohibited throughout the national territory. The simplest is to make the party 18+: put it in the name, in the first line of the description and as a required question at checkout. At the dock, ID first and QR second. The full check is in 18+ only events.

Each purchase allows up to four tickets of each type, so a large group buys in two goes. And with ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set: the service and processing fees are paid by the buyer, with the breakdown visible before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell more tickets than the boat's capacity?

No. The vessel's license sets the capacity, guests included. Subtract crew and team, and split the rest among the ticket types: at ENTRÁ each type has its own quota and the sum can't exceed that number.

How do I sell several sailings on the same day?

ENTRÁ doesn't have calendar time slots: you set up one event per day and one ticket type per sailing, with the time in the name ("Sailing 18:00") and the vessel's capacity as quota. The scanner shows the type, but the time is checked by whoever greets people at the dock.

What happens if the sailing is called off due to bad weather?

Write the rule before selling: rescheduling, refund or letting people choose. If you reschedule, you edit the date and notify people yourself. If you refund, you issue it from Mercado Pago and mark the ticket as refunded in the dashboard: the place goes back into the quota.

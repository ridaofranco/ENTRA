Guide for organizers

Exclusive presale for your followers: link, code and announcement

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 7 min read

The people who have followed you for a while or already bought a ticket from you are the first to respond when you announce a date. Giving them the first chance, at a better price and before everyone else, organizes your sales: money comes in early, you see whether the date takes off and you give your audience a concrete reason to stay tuned. This guide explains how to close that presale so only your people see it, how it is cut off in ENTRÁ, how long it should last and how to announce it.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

What an exclusive presale is

It is a batch of tickets sold before general sales, with a small capacity and, almost always, a lower price, that only one group can access: your followers, the buyer list from the previous date, your newsletter subscribers or the series' WhatsApp group. It has three pieces worth defining before touching the dashboard:

  • Who gets in: a specific list, not "anyone who sees the story".
  • How many: the tier's capacity, which is what makes it truly exclusive. If there are tickets for everyone, it is not a presale, it is general sales with another name.
  • Until when: a closing date and time you state from the first message.

If you haven't decided yet how many tiers you will have or how big the price jumps are, start with how to price your tickets: tiers and presale.

Two ways to close it: link or code

ENTRÁ has two tools to keep the presale from being grabbed by just anyone. They work alone or together.

  • Hidden event (link only). When creating or editing the event there is an "Evento oculto" (hidden event) switch: the event doesn't appear in the public listings and only people with the direct link can get in. It keeps the date out of sight until you want it seen, and you send the link to your list. The limit is obvious: the link can be forwarded, so the real filter is the tier's capacity.
  • Discount code. In the event's discounts tab you create a code with a percentage or fixed amount, a maximum number of uses and a date until which it is valid (it expires at 23:59 that day, Argentina time). The event can be visible to everyone, but only people with the code get the presale price. With the usage cap you control how many tickets go out at that price even if the code gets around.

A combination that works well: hidden event during the presale, link sent only to your list, and when you launch general sales you turn the switch off so the event appears in the listings. How to think about a code is covered in discount codes for an event.

How the presale works in ENTRÁ

Each tier is a ticket type with a name, price and capacity. The ones you want to sell first are marked with the Early Bird button when loading the tickets. While the presale is open, on the event page:

  • Presale tickets can be bought.
  • The other types are visible but locked, labeled "Próxima tanda" (next tier). That way your people know what comes next is more expensive.

When you decide to close, the event dashboard shows the Lanzar Venta Oficial (launch official sales) button (only if the event is already published and official sales haven't been launched). It asks you to confirm with the warning "Esto habilitará todos los tickets para el público" (this will enable all tickets for the public). When you accept:

  • Presale tickets are marked "Finalizado" (ended) and no longer sell, even if there was capacity left.
  • The other types are enabled for purchase.
  • The button disappears. There is no way back to the presale from the dashboard.

Two things to keep in mind before loading:

  • You make the cut. There is no automatic close by date or when capacity runs out: if you announced the presale ends on Thursday at 8 pm, at that time you have to go in and press the button.
  • Watch the names. Besides the Early Bird button, ENTRÁ treats as presale any types whose name includes words like "preventa", "anticipada", "early", "promo" or "lote". If you call a ticket you want to sell in general sales "Promo 2x1", it will behave like a presale. Check the names before publishing.

How long it lasts

An exclusive presale works because it is short and has an announced end. For your own list, one to three days is usually enough, or until capacity runs out, whichever comes first. If you stretch it over several weeks, whoever received it leaves the purchase for later and the presale blurs into general sales.

Some guidelines for setting the period:

  • Start on a day and at a time when your people are looking at their phones, not at dawn on a Sunday.
  • If you use a code, set the expiry date to match the announced close, so it doesn't stay alive after general sales launch.
  • Leave a margin for the event review. Before publishing, the ENTRÁ team reviews the event, usually within the day. Don't announce the presale for an hour from now if you haven't loaded it yet.
  • If it sells out in minutes, don't open another presale on top: launch official sales and ride the momentum.

To plan the full calendar, from the announcement to the day of the event, see when to open ticket sales.

How to announce it

The presale doesn't sell itself: if your list doesn't hear about it in time, the tier ends up half sold. The order we recommend:

  • Your buyer base first. From the dashboard you export buyers from previous dates. Write only to those who agreed to receive news, with a short message. How to build and look after that list is in your buyer base for the next date.
  • Then your closed channels: WhatsApp group or channel, Instagram close friends, newsletter. That is where the hidden event link or the code goes.
  • Only then, open promotion, when you launch general sales. In that announcement you can say the presale sold out: it is the best advertising you have.

The presale message has to say, plainly:

  • What it is: date, venue and who plays or what happens.
  • Why it reaches that person: "because you came to the last one" or "because you are in the group".
  • How many tickets there are and at what price, and how much they will cost later.
  • Until when, with day and time.
  • The link or the code, and that it is personal.

A possible message, to adapt:

"Hi, we are writing because you came to the August date. On December 14 we are back at the same venue, and before announcing it we are opening 80 tickets at presale price for those who already came. They are valid until Thursday at 8 pm or until they run out. After that the price goes up. Link: [event link]."

A reminder on the last day, a few hours before closing, usually moves those who left it for later. More ideas about those channels in selling tickets through Instagram and WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make the presale visible only to my followers?

You have two routes. You mark the event as hidden, so it doesn't appear in the listings and only people with the link can get in, and you send that link to your list. Or you leave the event visible and create a discount code with limited uses and an expiry date, which you pass only to your people. They can be combined.

Can the Lanzar Venta Oficial button be undone?

Not from the dashboard. When you press it, presale tickets are marked as ended and the other types are enabled. Press it when you are sure you want to close the presale, because there is no automatic cut by date.

How long should an exclusive presale last?

Briefly, with an announced end. For your own list one to three days is enough, or until capacity runs out. If it lasts weeks it loses its urgency and blends into general sales.

The event was cancelled or rescheduled: what happens to your ticket

By SOMOS DER · July 29, 2026 · 6 min read

It happens, and it happens often: you bought a ticket and the event falls through, gets moved to another date, or you simply can't make it that day anymore. These are the three situations and what applies in each one.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

1. The event was cancelled

The refund policy is set and communicated by the event organizer, not by the platform where you bought. ENTRÁ acts as a technology intermediary between the organizer and the audience: it provides the tools to issue the ticket, take payment and control access, but organizing and running the event is solely the organizer's responsibility.

That doesn't leave you without backing. In Argentina, Argentina's Law 24.240 on Consumer Protection applies and its rights are yours all the same. If you bought through ENTRÁ, write to us at contacto@somosder.com.ar and we will help you with the process with the organizer.

What to do, in order:

  1. Keep the purchase email and the payment receipt. That is your proof.
  2. Look for the organizer's official announcement: it usually includes the deadline and the channel for requesting a refund.
  3. If there is no announcement or they don't answer, write to us with the transaction number.
  4. Note down the date of each contact. If there is a formal complaint later, that timeline counts.

2. The event was rescheduled

A rescheduling is not a cancellation. Your ticket is normally still valid for the new date and you don't have to do anything.

If the new date doesn't work for you, you have two options:

  • Ask the organizer for a refund, according to the policy they announced for the rescheduling.
  • Transfer the ticket to someone else, if the organizer has transfers enabled.

3. You can't go: transfer the ticket, not the screenshot

This is the most common mistake and the most expensive one. Sending a screenshot of the QR over WhatsApp transfers nothing. It just creates one more copy of the same code, and the code is single use: whoever arrives first gets in. If that is someone else, your friend is left outside holding a screenshot and there is nobody to complain to.

The official transfer inside the platform works differently: when you transfer, the old code is invalidated and a new QR is generated for the person receiving it. The old screenshot stops working automatically. That is exactly what prevents duplication and fraud.

How it works in ENTRÁ:

  1. You go to your profile and choose the ticket you want to transfer.
  2. A transfer link is generated for the recipient.
  3. That person claims it and receives their new QR, in their name.
  4. Your old code is invalidated.

If you change your mind and it hasn't been claimed yet, you can cancel the transfer: the link stops working and the ticket is yours again and can be transferred again.

Transfers depend on the organizer having them enabled for that event, and on the ticket being valid and unused.

Why not to buy or sell outside the platform

Reselling outside the platform is prohibited under ENTRÁ's terms, and it is not just a formality: it is the only way to protect yourself.

When you buy a ticket outside the platform, you are buying an image of a code. There is no way to know whether that same image has already been sold five times, whether the code was already used, or whether the ticket was cancelled or refunded. At the door, the system validates the code: if someone already got in with it, access is denied. Whoever paid outside is left out, and there is no possible claim.

If you are an organizer: settle this before selling

Three decisions that save you a problem the day it happens:

  • Write your refund policy for cancellation and for rescheduling, and publish it alongside the sale. Don't improvise it afterwards.
  • Decide whether to enable ticket transfers. Enabling them cuts informal resale a lot, because it gives your audience a legitimate path.
  • Have a visible contact channel. Most complaints escalate because of silence, not because of the money.

Frequently asked questions

If an event is cancelled, do I get my ticket money back?

The refund policy is set and communicated by the event organizer, not by the ticketing platform: ENTRÁ acts as a technology intermediary and running the event is solely the organizer's responsibility. In Argentina you are also covered by the rights in Argentina's Law 24.240 on Consumer Protection. If you bought through ENTRÁ and need to pursue a claim, write to us at contacto@somosder.com.ar and we will help you through the process with the organizer.

The event was moved to another date and I can't go. What can I do?

A rescheduling is not the same as a cancellation: your ticket is normally still valid for the new date. If you can't go, you have two options: ask the organizer for a refund according to the policy they announced, or transfer the ticket to someone else from the platform if the organizer enabled transfers.

How do I pass my ticket to someone else without a copy floating around?

By using the official transfer inside the platform. When you transfer it, the old code is invalidated and a new QR is generated for the recipient. That prevents duplication: the old screenshot stops working. Sending the screenshot over WhatsApp transfers nothing, it just multiplies copies of the same code.

Can I cancel a transfer I already sent?

Yes, as long as the other person hasn't claimed it yet. When you cancel it, the link stops working and the ticket is yours again and can be transferred again.

Buying or selling tickets?

At ENTRÁ each ticket is a unique QR that can only change hands through the official transfer inside the platform, which invalidates the old code and issues a new one. If you have a problem with a purchase, write to contacto@somosder.com.ar.

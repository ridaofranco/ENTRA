Guide for organizers

Fair or market with tickets: capacity, time slots and entry control

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 5 min read

A design fair, a producers' market, a small weekend expo: lots of people coming and going, stalls that take up space and a peak crowd nobody planned for. This guide covers how to decide whether to charge for entry, how to think about capacity and how to set up time slots with ENTRÁ, which has no booking slots feature but does have ticket types with their own capacity.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

Paid entry or free with reservation

The first thing is deciding whether to charge for entry. Both options work, for different things:

  • Paid general admission. It covers part of the costs (venue, setup, security) and filters out the casual passerby. It makes sense when the fair has a curation people seek out.
  • Free with reservation. The public doesn't pay, but gets a ticket with a QR. It helps you know how many people are coming, keep capacity from being exceeded and keep the audience's data, which you can later show exhibitors.

If it is free, reservations have a known problem: some of the people who reserve don't show up. That is why it can make sense to release a few more reservations than capacity, but only if the venue really has room. In free event: how to stop people reserving and not coming you will find how to lower that number.

How many people fit at once

At a fair people don't all arrive together or stay all day: they come in, walk around and leave. That changes the math. The number that matters is how many people can be inside at the same time, which comes from the venue's licensed capacity minus what the stalls, exhibitors and staff take up. Ask the venue for that number in writing before opening sales.

A necessary clarification: the ENTRÁ scanner records who comes in, not who leaves. It has no real time counter of people inside. If the fair can fill up at a peak, put someone with a hand counter at the exit, or handle it with time slots as explained below.

Time slots: how to do it with ticket types

ENTRÁ does not have a booking slot or time per ticket feature. What it does have is ticket types with their own name and capacity, and that is enough to set up slots:

  • Create one type per slot, with the time in the name: "Sábado 11 a 14" (Saturday 11 to 2), "Sábado 14 a 17", "Sábado 17 a 20".
  • Give each type a capacity equal to what fits in the venue at once. When a slot fills up, that type closes on its own and the rest stay on sale.
  • The buyer picks their slot the same way they pick any ticket type, paid or free.

What you need to know: the scanner shows each QR's ticket type, but it does not reject someone for arriving at a different time. The slot is checked by the person at the door, looking at the type name that appears on scanning. Decide in advance what you do with latecomers (let them in if there is room, or ask them to wait) and write it in the event description.

If the fair runs for several days, you can also load it as a multi day event and choose whether the QR is valid for every day (once per day) or the buyer picks which days to attend.

Exhibitors are not the public

Stalls, their helpers and staff shouldn't come out of the same capacity as the public without you knowing. Two ways to organize it:

  • Complimentary tickets from the dashboard, with name, email and reason ("Expositor", exhibitor), so each one has their own QR and goes through the scanner without mixing with sales.
  • A separate list of exhibitors at the door, if you'd rather they not go through ENTRÁ. In that case subtract those people from capacity by hand.

If the fair has badge based accreditation, corporate guests or a business expo, that is a different thing: for that SOMOS DER has PASE (pase.somosder.ar).

The door

  • Scanning from a phone, with a QR valid only once (or once per day, if it runs several days).
  • If someone can't find their QR, you look it up by last name, ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID) or email.
  • A sign with the event link for people arriving without a ticket, as long as sales are open and there is capacity.

Frequently asked questions

Does ENTRÁ have booking slots or time slots?

Not as a built in feature. You handle it with one ticket type per slot, with the time in the name and its own capacity: when a slot fills up, it closes on its own. The scanner shows each QR's type, but the time is checked by the person at the door.

Can I run a free fair and still have people get tickets?

Yes. The event can be free with reservation: the public doesn't pay and still gets a ticket with a QR. That way you know how many people are coming and protect the venue's capacity.

Does the scanner tell me how many people are inside?

No. It records who comes in, not who leaves, so it is not a counter of people inside. If the venue can fill up at a peak, count exits by hand or spread the public across time slots.

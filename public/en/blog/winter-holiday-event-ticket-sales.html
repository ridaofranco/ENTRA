Winter school holidays in Argentina: selling tickets show by show

By SOMOS DER · October 31, 2026 · 6 min read

In July (winter in Argentina) families look for something to do with their kids every afternoon, for two weeks. It is the shortest and most intense season for children's plays, workshops, puppets, magic and activities with limited spots. To sell it well you need to sort out three things before opening: when the holidays fall where you will hold the event, how to load many shows and which tickets each family will buy.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

Dates change by province

Winter holidays are not the same across the country: each province sets its own break. For reference, in the 2026 school calendar published by the Consejo Federal de Educación (Federal Education Council, in Spanish) there were three waves:

  • July 6 to 17: Córdoba, Entre Ríos, Mendoza, San Juan, San Luis and Santa Fe.
  • July 13 to 24: Catamarca, Chubut, Corrientes, Formosa, Jujuy, La Pampa, La Rioja, Misiones, Neuquén, Río Negro, Salta, Santa Cruz, Tierra del Fuego and Tucumán.
  • July 20 to 31: Buenos Aires Province, the City of Buenos Aires, Chaco and Santiago del Estero.

For next year, wait for your province to publish its calendar and don't plan anything with last year's dates. Three things come out of that table:

  • Your season lasts as long as your province's break, not all of July. A play in Rosario and another in La Plata don't have the same two weeks.
  • If you tour, the calendar builds your route. With a traveling show, the staggered waves let you do one province per week without overlap. How to set up a tour across several cities is in selling tickets for a tour.
  • Tourist destinations get people from other provinces, with other dates. A ski resort or a hill town can have an audience for more weeks than the local break.

Many shows: one event per show

During the holidays it is normal to have a show every day, sometimes two a day. The temptation is to load a single long event. In ENTRÁ the tidy way is one event per show, for concrete reasons:

  • Each show has its own capacity. If Saturday's sells out, Tuesday's stays on sale.
  • Each show has its own link, which you can send on its own when spots remain.
  • Each show has its own door. The scanner rejects tickets from another event: whoever bought for Wednesday doesn't get in on Tuesday.

ENTRÁ has no time slots inside a single event. It has multi day events, with one QR for the whole event or one per day, but each ticket type's capacity is a single number for all days. That works for a week long fair where people circulate, not for a room with numbered seats. The step by step for a play with holiday shows is in children's play during the holidays.

For a workshop or an activity with few spots (ceramics, cooking, science, circus), the logic is the same: each session or each group is an event with the capacity of the table or the room. If it is a multi day workshop with the same group, a single event with "one QR for the whole event" works well, because the kids are the same every day. Child and adult tickets

At a holiday activity almost nobody comes alone. Split people into ticket types, each with its own name, price and capacity:

  • "Chico, desde 2 años" (child, from age 2), with the paying age in the name.
  • "Adulto" (adult), for whoever comes along. If adults don't go into a workshop, say so and don't sell it.
  • Babies: decide beforehand whether they pay, whether they sit on a lap and whether you want them counted. If you want them registered without charging, you can issue them a complimentary ticket from the dashboard.

Each purchase allows up to 4 tickets of each type, so a family with two adults and three kids fits in a single purchase. If you need to know something about the kids (ages, allergies for a cooking workshop), you can add extra questions to the purchase form; they are answered once per purchase, not per ticket, so ask for "kids' ages" in a single question. When to open sales

During winter holidays many families decide in the morning what to do in the afternoon, but others plan the two weeks ahead, especially if parents work and share the days with grandparents. To reach both:

  • Open a few weeks ahead, once you know your province's break dates. June is a good time to announce.
  • Publish the best selling shows first: weekends and afternoons. Add morning or weekday shows as you see sales.
  • Use a short presale as a hook. In ENTRÁ the types marked as Early Bird are sold during the presale, and you make the cut yourself with "Lanzar Venta Oficial" (launch official sale); there is no automatic switch by date, so announce how long it lasts and stick to it.
  • Load early. Each event goes through a review before being published, usually the same day. With fifteen shows, load them before the announcement.
  • Keep sales open until the start. During the holidays many people buy on the way. The page sells until the end time you loaded; if you don't load one, until three hours after the start.

What to say on each show's page

  • Which ages it is for and how long it lasts.
  • Doors and start time, separately. With kids and coats, fifteen minutes before is just right.
  • Whether the venue is heated and where coats and strollers go.
  • What happens if the child gets sick. In July it happens often. Say whether you accept changes or refunds and how; if you have transfers enabled, the family can pass the ticket to another email from their profile.

ENTRÁ automatically sends an email the day before each show with the date, time and venue. If you change something later, editing the event does not notify buyers by email: you send the notice yourself with the buyer list, which you export as a CSV from the dashboard.

The door during the holidays

Families arrive together, cold and in a hurry. Scan all of a family's QRs in a row from the same phone; if someone can't find the email, they are looked up by last name, ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID) or email from the scanner. ENTRÁ has no box office and does not record cash: whoever arrives without a ticket buys from their phone, with a sign showing the QR of the show's link away from the scanning line. With ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set for each ticket; service and processing fees are paid by the buyer, with the breakdown visible before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Are winter holidays on the same dates across the country?

No. Each province sets its own break. In the 2026 school calendar published by the Consejo Federal de Educación there were three waves: July 6 to 17, July 13 to 24 and July 20 to 31. Before planning your shows, check the calendar of the province where you will hold the event.

Should I load the two weeks of holidays as a single event?

It is not a good idea. In ENTRÁ the tidy way is one event per show: each one has its own link, capacity, list and door control, and the scanner rejects tickets from another show. If there are two shows on the same day, they are two events.

When should I open sales for winter holiday shows?

A few weeks ahead, once you know your province's break dates. Publish the best selling shows first, like weekends and afternoons, and add the rest as sales move.

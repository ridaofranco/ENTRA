Guide for organizers

Answering buyer questions: ready-to-copy replies

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 7 min read

When you start selling, the messages start: on Instagram, on WhatsApp, at any hour. Almost all of them are the same five questions. If you have the answers ready, you reply in a minute, you don't get wrong what the platform does or doesn't do, and the buyer is reassured. Here they are, ready to copy and adjust to your event, with the full guide for each case to send when more detail is needed.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

Before replying: three rules

  • Reply fast, even if it is short. An "I'll look into it" five minutes later is worth more than a perfect answer the next day. Whoever gets no answer buys again "just in case" or complains in the comments.
  • Don't ask for data you don't need. To find a purchase, name, email and order number are enough. Never ask for a screenshot of the QR code: that code is their access and it shouldn't be floating around chats.
  • Promise only what depends on you. If you decide on refunds, say so. If a refund takes as long as the bank takes, don't say "you'll have it tomorrow".

"My ticket never arrived"

It is the most common question and the ticket almost always exists. What happens is that it is in another folder or the payment hasn't cleared yet. The ticket is issued when the payment is confirmed; the email goes out with the subject "Tu entrada para [event]" (Spanish for "Your ticket for"), with the QR code in the body and an attached PDF. Also, the ticket stays in the buyer's profile, in Mis Tickets (my tickets), even if they bought without creating an account, because ENTRÁ looks tickets up by email.

Reply to copy:

"Hi! Search Spam and Promotions for the event name or the word ENTRÁ. If it doesn't show up, go to entratickets.com, log in or create an account with the same email you used to buy and open Mis Tickets: the ticket is there with the QR code. If your payment is pending, the ticket is issued automatically when it clears; don't buy again in the meantime. If you think you typed your email wrong, send me your name, ID number (DNI), the order number (Orden #…) and the correct email."

If they typed the email wrong, support fixes it by hand: they should message WhatsApp at +54 11 7154-0675 or write to soporte@entratickets.com with those details. On your side, in the dashboard you can see whether the ticket email was sent or the delivery failed. The full step by step, to send as is, is in you paid and your ticket never reached your email: what to do.

"How do I pass my ticket to a friend?"

If you have transfers enabled for the event (it is an option you choose when loading it), the answer is the official transfer from the profile. When the friend claims it, they get a new QR code in their name and the old one stops working. That is why a screenshot doesn't work: if two people have the same one, the first one gets in.

Reply to copy:

"Go to your profile on entratickets.com, choose the ticket and tap transfer. Enter your friend's name and email. They get a link by email (you can also copy it and send it on WhatsApp), they open it, log in with that same email and the ticket becomes theirs, with a new QR code. Don't send them a screenshot of the QR code: the code is valid only once. If you got the email wrong, you can cancel it from your profile as long as it hasn't been claimed."

If transfers are turned off, the button doesn't appear and there is no way to pass it on through the platform. Say so clearly. The option is chosen when loading the event: if you left it off by mistake, check with ENTRÁ support. The cases where a ticket can't be transferred (past event, transfer pending) are in how to transfer your ticket to someone else safely.

"Can I get a refund?"

This answer depends on you, because the refund policy is set by the organizer. Ideally it is written in the description before you sell. There are two different situations:

  • The buyer can't go. The simplest way out is a transfer: the ticket isn't lost and you don't refund money.
  • The event is cancelled. When you cancel it on the platform, a notice email goes to all buyers, and the refund is handled with support. If it is rescheduled, the ticket remains valid for the new date.

Reply to copy (adjust it to your policy):

"Hi! If you can't come, the fastest option is to transfer the ticket to someone else from your profile. [If you refund: If you'd rather have a refund, send me your name and order number and I'll handle it; the money goes back to the same payment method and the timing depends on your bank or card.] If the event is called off, we'll let you know by email."

When you refund, mark it in the dashboard right away: the ticket shows as refunded, the scanner rejects it at the door and the spot goes back on sale. If you refund the money and don't mark it, the QR code is still live. If they ask about the right of withdrawal and what the law says, send them can you return a ticket bought online?, which quotes the official texts without playing lawyer.

"Can I pay cash?"

Two things need to be separated. Online, payment goes through Mercado Pago, and the buyer chooses among the methods the checkout offers. If one of those methods is pending, the ticket is issued only once it is approved. At the door, ENTRÁ has no box office and does not record cash payments: what does work is buying online from a phone in the line, while sales are still open.

Reply to copy:

"Tickets are bought online at [link], with the payment methods Mercado Pago offers you. No need to create an account: enter your name and email, pay, and the ticket shows on screen and reaches you by email. [If you sell at the door: You can also buy it from your phone at the door, but it costs more than the advance ticket.]"

If you decide to accept cash in hand, that money stays outside the system and you keep track of it yourself. To let the person in with a record, you can issue them a complimentary ticket from the dashboard with their name and email, but it will show as complimentary, not as a sale. Use it as an exception. The whole door setup is in selling tickets at the door: advance or door sales, and how to do it.

"Until what time can I get in?"

The platform doesn't cut off entry: you decide that with the venue. What it does cut off is online sales, which stay open until the end time you loaded or, if you didn't load one, until three hours after the start. If your event runs longer and you want to sell late, load the end time. If the venue has an entry cutoff, say so in the description and on the flyer.

Reply to copy:

"Doors open at [time] and [artist or the main thing] starts at [time]. You can get in until [cutoff time]. Bring the QR code on your phone (the PDF from the email works too) and your ID if we ask for it."

And for whoever asks what happens if their battery dies or they don't have the email: at the door they can be looked up by last name, ID number or email from the scanner. It is worth saying, because it lowers anxiety and shortens the line.

So they don't ask you the same thing three times

Every repeated question is a line missing from the event description. It is edited from the dashboard, so you can add it on the spot. The minimum it should say:

  • Whether tickets can be transferred and how.
  • Your refund policy and what happens if the event is called off or rescheduled.
  • Whether there are door sales, at what price, and that they are online from a phone.
  • What time doors open, when it starts and until when people can get in.
  • A contact channel you actually check.

Save these replies as quick replies in WhatsApp Business or in a note on your phone, with the event link already in place. Next time someone writes to you at one in the morning, you reply in ten seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What do I reply to someone who says their ticket never arrived?

To search Spam and Promotions for the event name or the word ENTRÁ, and to go to their profile, in Mis Tickets (my tickets), with the same email they used to buy: the ticket appears there even if they bought without an account. If they typed the email wrong, they should send their name, ID number (DNI), order number and the correct email to ENTRÁ support.

Can my buyers pay cash at the door?

ENTRÁ has no box office and does not record cash payments. What you can offer is that they buy online from their phone in the line, while sales are still open. If you accept cash on the side, that money stays outside the system and you keep track of it yourself.

Where do I put the answers so people don't keep asking the same thing?

In the event description, which is edited from the dashboard. If three people ask you the same question, the answer goes there: refund policy, whether tickets can be transferred, what time doors open and whether there are door sales.

Fewer questions, fewer problems at the door

In ENTRÁ the ticket stays in the buyer's profile, it is transferred with a new QR code and at the door it can be looked up by last name, ID number (DNI) or email. Creating the account and loading the event costs nothing.

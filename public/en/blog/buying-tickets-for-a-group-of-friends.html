Guide for buyers

Buying tickets for a group of friends

By SOMOS DER · November 25, 2026 · 6 min read

If several of you are going to the same gig or party, there are two ways to do it: one person buys all the tickets, or each person buys their own. Both work. What decides which one suits you is who will be at the door with which QR code. If one person buys everything, the key is to share the tickets the official way, not by screenshot, so that on the day each person can get in on their own.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). This guide describes how group purchases and ticket transfers work on ENTRÁ.

When it makes sense for one person to buy

Buying everything together makes sense in these cases:

  • Tickets will sell fast. If the release is small or the date sells out quickly, one person at checkout is faster than five people coordinating in a group chat.
  • You want the same ticket type. If there is a cheaper release, buying together means nobody misses that price for showing up ten minutes late.
  • Someone can't pay online. One person pays and you settle it among yourselves later.

One limit to keep in mind: on ENTRÁ, the event page lets you add up to 4 tickets of each type per purchase, as long as they are still available. If there are six of you, that is two purchases or two people buying.

When it makes sense for each person to buy their own

  • You will arrive at different times. If one arrives at 10 pm and another at 1 am, each needs their own QR code on their own phone, in their own name.
  • There is no rush. If plenty of tickets are left, everyone paying for themselves avoids the classic «I'll pay you back later» that never happens.
  • The group is big. More than four of the same type means splitting the purchase anyway.

Each purchase carries the details of the person paying (name, email and ID number, DNI in Argentina), and the service fee is shown at checkout before you confirm, whether one person buys or everyone does.

What you get if you bought several

Once the payment is approved, each ticket comes with its own QR code. You have them in three places:

  • In the confirmation email, with the QR codes inside the message and a PDF attached with all the tickets in the purchase.
  • On the confirmation screen, right after you pay.
  • In Mis Tickets (my tickets), inside your profile, with the button to download them.

One detail to watch: all those tickets are in your name, because you are the buyer. Until you share them, you are the holder of all five.

How to share them without screenshots

The temptation is to send a screenshot of each QR code to the group chat. The problem is that each QR code is valid only once, the screenshot gets forwarded, someone uses it first and at the door your friend shows up with a ticket that has already been used.

What works is the official transfer, one per friend:

  • Go to Mis Tickets (my tickets) in your profile and choose one of the tickets.
  • Enter your friend's name, email and phone number.
  • They get a link by email to claim it, and you can also send it yourself on WhatsApp.
  • Your friend logs in with that same email and claims it. At that moment a new QR code is issued in their name and yours is invalidated.

If you typed the wrong email, you can cancel the transfer from the same place while it is still pending: the ticket goes back to you and the link stops working.

Two things can block a transfer: the event has already taken place, or the organizer has disabled transfers for that event. In that case you will have to go to the door together or sort it out with the organizer.

On the day of the event

  • If you shared the tickets: everyone gets in with their own QR code, at their own time. Nobody waits for anybody on the sidewalk.
  • If you didn't: you need to be at the door with all the QR codes, and the group gets in with you. If you are late, everyone is late.
  • If someone forgets their phone: door staff can look up the purchase by name, email or ID number. If the ticket was never transferred, it is in your name, so the search is done with your details.

A tip from the door: have everyone download or save their ticket before leaving home. With weak signal in the queue, opening an email takes forever.

If someone drops out

It always happens: someone pulls out at the last minute. If you still hold their ticket, transfer it to whoever is going to use it. If you had already passed it on, they have to transfer it from their own profile. Avoid selling the screenshot to a stranger: besides the risk that the QR code has already been used, the person buying a screenshot has no way of knowing if it is valid.

Frequently asked questions

How many tickets can I buy in a single purchase on ENTRÁ?

On the event page you can add up to 4 tickets of each type per purchase, as long as they are still available. If the group is bigger, make a second purchase or have each person buy their own. Every ticket comes with its own QR code.

I bought everyone's tickets. How do I pass them on to my friends?

The safest way is the official transfer from Mis Tickets (my tickets) in your profile: you enter each friend's name, email and phone number and they get a link to claim their ticket. When they claim it with that email, a new QR code is issued in their name and yours is invalidated. That way everyone gets in on their own and nobody depends on a screenshot.

Can my friends get in with a screenshot of the QR code I sent them on WhatsApp?

Technically the scanner reads the code, but each QR code is valid only once: if the screenshot was passed around and someone used it first, it will show as already used at the door. Also, the ticket is still in your name, so if there is a problem you are the one who has to be there. That is why the official transfer is the better option.

If a friend can't make it in the end, what do I do with their ticket?

If you still hold it, you can transfer it from Mis Tickets (my tickets) to someone else in the group or to whoever will use it. If you had already transferred it to them, they are the one who has to pass it on from their profile. Tickets cannot be transferred once the event has taken place or if the organizer has disabled transfers for that event.

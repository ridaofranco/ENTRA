A bus to a concert or festival in another city: how to sell the trip

By SOMOS DER · October 31, 2026 · 7 min read

A band plays in Córdoba, Rosario or La Plata and in your city there are a hundred people who want to go but have no way to get back at three in the morning. If you put a bus together, you have a small, tidy business, as long as it is clear what you are selling and what you are not.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

What you sell is the seat on the bus

The concert ticket is sold by the show's ticketing platform. You sell something else: a seat on a bus that leaves at a set time, goes to a place and comes back. If that isn't clear, someone buys the bus thinking it includes the ticket.

To prevent it:

  • The event name says it: "Round trip bus to X's concert in Rosario". And so does the ticket type name: "Bus seat (does not include show ticket)".
  • The first line of the description repeats it: "This is transport only. The concert ticket is bought separately, on the show's official ticketing platform".

Trip only or a package with the ticket?

There are two ways to set it up:

  • Trip only. Each person buys their show ticket on their own and buys the bus from you. It is the simplest: you don't handle third party tickets, you don't depend on another platform and the price is easy to explain.
  • "Bus + ticket" package. You load it as a separate ticket type, with the total price and a name that says what it includes. But ENTRÁ only issues the bus QR code: you have to get the concert ticket yourself and deliver it through the platform that issued it. Before offering it, check that platform's terms to see whether tickets can be passed to someone else and how, because many prohibit resale. And write on the page when and how that ticket will arrive.

If you offer both options, set up the trip only and the package as separate types, each with its own capacity, and make sure the total doesn't exceed the seats on the bus.

The capacity is the seats

A bus has a number of seats and nobody travels standing. The capacity comes from there:

  • Seats on the bus you hired, minus those taken by your team (the coordinator who travels, for example).
  • If there are several buses, one ticket type per bus: "Bus 1", "Bus 2", each with its seats.
  • If there is more than one pickup point, you don't need a type per stop: in ENTRÁ each type has its own capacity and doesn't share it, so you would end up with the bus split into pieces. Better a required multiple choice question at purchase: "Where are you getting on?", with each stop and its time.

Each purchase allows up to four seats of each type. A group of six buys in two goes.

The passenger list with ID numbers

The passenger list is not a whim of yours. Argentina's national transport regulator (CNRT), in Disposition 1011/2018, points out that drawing up and carrying the passenger list is an obligation of tourist road transport companies, based on article 16 of Decree 958/92. The bus company prepares that list, but you give them the data. That is why it is worth hiring a licensed company and asking them beforehand what data they need and by when.

How to collect it in ENTRÁ:

  • Checkout asks for the buyer's name, email and ID number (DNI). But that DNI is one per purchase: if someone buys four seats, you have four QR codes and a single ID.
  • To get every passenger's, when creating the event add a required long text question: "Full name and DNI of each person traveling". The buyer can't pay without filling it in.
  • Export the attendee list from the dashboard: it comes out as a CSV, opens fine in Excel and includes name, email, DNI, phone, ticket type and one column per question. That spreadsheet, tidied up, is what you give the company.
  • Close sales in time. If the company needs the list the day before, from the event settings you switch it to "Pausado" (paused) and nobody else can buy. If you don't pause it, the page sells until the end time you entered.

If minors are traveling without their parents, ask beforehand for an authorization signed by a responsible adult and ask the transport company what documents it requires. And handle the list with care: it is your audience's personal data, to be used for the trip and nothing else. Pickup, return and the person who doesn't show up

The problem is the way back, leaving the show with a dead battery. Write everything down before selling:

  • Pickup time and place for each stop, with the exact address and the rule: "The bus leaves 15 minutes after the scheduled time and does not wait any longer".
  • Where the bus parks at the destination and how to walk there from the show's exit.
  • The return time, tied to the end of the show: "We leave 45 minutes after the last artist finishes". And how long you wait for someone who doesn't turn up: if the bus leaves after fifteen minutes, say so.

At boarding, the coordinator scans the QR codes from their phone, with the ENTRÁ scanner in the browser. Each QR is valid only once: if someone passed on a screenshot, whoever arrives first gets on. If someone can't find the email, they can be looked up by surname, ID number or email, and if there is no signal on the road, check-ins are saved on the phone. The scanner records a single check-in per QR, so on the way back the check is manual: the coordinator does a headcount with the exported list.

If the festival lasts several days and you sell a bus for the whole weekend, the way of getting in (one QR for the whole event or one QR per day) is chosen once when creating the event. If each day is a different trip, with different people, the clearest option is one event per day. How to decide between a full pass and a single day is in full pass or day ticket.

If the show is called off

It happens: rain, problems with the band, a venue that doesn't get its permit. And you have a bus hired that may no longer be cancellable. That is why the rule is written before selling, on the event page:

  • If the show is moved to another date, the trip moves with it. You edit the event date in ENTRÁ and the tickets remain valid. The change doesn't send a notice on its own: write to each buyer using the email and phone from the list. The day before reminder goes out with whatever date is loaded.
  • If the show is cancelled before departure, say what you refund: everything, or everything minus what the bus company won't give back to you. Work it out with the company beforehand, so you don't promise what you can't deliver.
  • If it is called off with the bus already at the destination, the transport was provided: say whether there is a refund or not.

You refund each seat from Mercado Pago, and then in the dashboard you mark the ticket as returned: the seat goes back to the capacity and an email is prepared to let the person know. The show ticket is refunded by the concert's ticketing platform, not by you: write that down too. If someone can't travel on the new date and transfers are enabled, they can pass their seat to someone else from their profile, with a new QR code. More in the event was cancelled or rescheduled.

With ENTRÁ you receive 100% of the price you set for each seat. The service and processing fees are paid by the buyer, visible before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell the concert ticket together with the bus?

The show ticket is sold by the concert's ticketing platform. If you put together a package, you get the ticket yourself and deliver it through that platform, if its terms allow it. The simplest thing is to sell only the seat on the bus.

How do I build the passenger list with each person's ID number?

The DNI at checkout is the buyer's. Add a required long text question, "Name and DNI of each person traveling", and export the attendee list as a CSV: it has one column per question.

What happens with the bus if the concert is called off?

Whatever you wrote before selling. If the show moves to another date, so does the trip and the QR code remains valid. If it is cancelled, you refund everything or deduct what the bus company won't give back to you. The show ticket is refunded by the other ticketing platform.

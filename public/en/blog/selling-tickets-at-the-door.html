Selling tickets at the door: advance or door sales, and how to do it

By SOMOS DER · October 29, 2026 · 6 min read

Almost every small event asks the same question: do I sell everything beforehand or keep tickets for the door? Advance sales give you money and certainty; the door gives you the people who decide at the last minute. Here is how to think about it, what happens in the line when you sell at the door and how it works with ENTRÁ, which has no box office but does let you sell until the event starts.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

What each one gives you

Advance sales work for you before the event:

  • You know how many people are coming and can adjust bar, staff and security in time.
  • You have money before paying the day's expenses.
  • The door is faster: whoever already bought just shows the QR code.

The door captures what advance sales don't reach:

  • The person who decides that same night, the one passing by, the friend who joins at the last minute.
  • The audience that, out of habit, doesn't buy ahead.

The cost of the door is uncertainty (you don't know if they will come) and the line: each sale on the spot is slower than scanning a QR code, and if it mixes with those who already have tickets, it holds everyone up.

So, is it worth selling at the door?

It depends on your event, but there is one rule that almost never fails: advance tickets have to be cheaper than the door, and that has to be stated everywhere. If they cost the same, nobody has a reason to buy early and you are left without certainty. Some guidelines:

  • If capacity is small (a theater room, a workshop), sell almost everything in advance and leave the door for whatever is left.
  • If the audience is last minute (a party, a night with several bands), keep the door open, but at a higher price.
  • If the event can sell out, say in your promotion that the door is "only if available": it pushes advance sales.

What ENTRÁ has and what it doesn't

So as not to promise what isn't there: ENTRÁ has no box office. There is no screen to take cash or card terminal payments at the door, and ENTRÁ does not record payments that don't go through its checkout. What it does have helps you handle the door another way:

  • Online sales stay open during the event. The page sells until the end time you loaded. If you didn't load an end time, it keeps selling until three hours after the start, on purpose, so sales don't stop as soon as the doors open.
  • People buy without an account. The buyer enters name and email, pays and, when done, sees their ticket with the QR code on screen. It also reaches them by email.
  • Prices and types editable from the dashboard. You can change the price or capacity of a ticket type, and add new types with whatever name you want, while the event is on sale.
  • Scanner and search at the door. What is sold on the spot goes into the scanner like any other ticket, and if the QR code doesn't show up, the person is looked up by last name, ID number (DNI) or email.

How to set up the "door" with ENTRÁ

The idea is simple: door sales are online sales made from the line. Step by step:

  • Close advance sales at the time you decide. When the moment comes, in the dashboard you raise that ticket type's price, or mark it as sold out (lowering the total to what has already sold) and add a new type called "Puerta" (door) with its price. Careful: any type that is on sale shows on the event page for everyone, including whoever buys from home that night. If you want the door price to start at a certain time, change it at that time, not before.
  • Put up a sign with the QR code for the event link in the line, clearly visible and away from the scanner. Whoever doesn't have a ticket buys while waiting.
  • Split into two lines: one for those who already have a QR code and another, short one, for those who are buying. That way buying on a phone doesn't slow down scanning.
  • Check the venue's signal. To buy in the line, the buyer needs mobile data. And the scanner phone needs a connection to learn about tickets sold after its last sync. If the venue has poor signal, read what happens if the internet drops at the door.
  • Have someone on the search tool for whoever bought but can't find the email: with the last name or ID number you validate them anyway.

What if someone wants to pay cash?

It will happen. Since ENTRÁ doesn't record cash payments, that money stays outside the system and you keep track of it yourself. If you still want to let them in with a record, from the dashboard you can issue them a complimentary ticket with their name, email and a reason, and the QR code reaches them by email. But be clear that in your numbers it will show as complimentary, not as a sale, and that money doesn't appear in any ENTRÁ report. Use it as an exception, not as a system: if cash at the door is central to your event, you need another tool for that. For handling invitations in general, see complimentary tickets and invitations.

Frequently asked questions

Does ENTRÁ have a box office or cash sales at the door?

No. ENTRÁ has no box office module and does not record cash payments. What you can do is keep online sales open during the event and have people buy from their phones in the line, on the same event page.

Until when can tickets be bought online on the day of the event?

Until the end time you loaded for the event. If you didn't load an end time, the page keeps selling until three hours after the start, precisely so sales don't stop as soon as the doors open.

Can I charge more at the door than for advance tickets?

Yes, by changing the price from the dashboard when advance sales close, or by marking that ticket type as sold out and adding another one named "Puerta" (door) with a different price. Keep in mind that any ticket type on sale shows on the event page, including from outside the venue.

Does someone buying in the line have to create an account?

No. You can buy without an account, with name and email. When payment is done, the ticket with the QR code appears on the phone screen and also arrives by email, so they can go to the scanner right away.

Advance and door sales on the same page

In ENTRÁ sales stay open during the event, people buy without an account and each QR code is valid only once at the scanner. Creating the account and loading the event costs nothing.

Summer camp or summer workshop: sign-ups by the week

By SOMOS DER · October 30, 2026 · 6 min read

A summer camp (in Argentina, summer holidays run through January and February), a drama workshop for kids in January or a two week intensive course have something a regular workshop doesn't: they aren't sold per class, they are sold by the week or by the fortnight, and the same person comes every day. That changes how the event is loaded, how the cap is set up and what data you need to ask for. This guide is about that. The general setup of a sign-up with limited places, field by field, is in sign-ups for a workshop with limited places.

A note before you start: ENTRÁ is an Argentine ticketing platform. It sells tickets for events held in Argentina, priced in Argentine pesos (ARS). What this guide says applies to most events; when it describes how ENTRÁ works, it means in Argentina.

First: what unit you are selling

Before loading anything, decide what the family or student is buying:

  • By the week. The most common for camps: each week is a block and people build their summer by choosing weeks.
  • By the fortnight. Typical of intensive courses or camps that work with a two week program.
  • By shift within the week. Morning, afternoon or full day. This doesn't change the unit, but it does change price and cap.

If you mix units (single week and full season), think of each one as a separate product.

How to load it in ENTRÁ: one event per week

The approach that works best today is to load each week (or each fortnight) as a multi-day event:

  • Turn on the multi-day option and load each day with its date and time. You only load the days with activity: Monday to Friday, no weekends or public holidays.
  • Choose "one QR for the whole event". The registered child comes in every day of the week with the same QR, once per day: it can't be used twice on the same day.
  • Put the period in the title: "Summer camp, week of January 5 to 9". That is what appears on the ticket that arrives by email.
  • Repeat the event for each week. Each one has its own cap, list and sales, and a week that fills up doesn't hold back the others.

What to avoid: loading the whole summer as a single event with one ticket type per week. In one QR for the whole event mode, that QR is valid on every day loaded, so the scanner wouldn't tell apart someone who paid for the first week from someone who paid for the third. And ENTRÁ has no cap per day: the cap is set per ticket type for the whole event. If you need an exact limit per week, the week has to be an event.

If you also want to sell single days, there is a "one QR per day" mode, where the buyer chooses the days and pays per day. Keep in mind that there the cap counts sign-ups, not places per day: with fixed places per day, it doesn't work for controlling the limit.

Ticket types: ages, shifts and presale

Within each week, each ticket type has a name, price and cap, and closes on its own when it fills up. That is where what is specific to a camp gets handled:

  • Groups by age. "Ages 4 to 6", "Ages 7 to 9", "Ages 10 to 12", each with the number of places you can handle with the staff you have for that group.
  • Shifts. "Morning", "Afternoon", "Full day", if price or cap change.
  • Presale. Each ticket type can be marked as presale, with its own price and cap, to reward those who sign up first.
  • Discounts for siblings or for those taking several weeks, with codes you hand out yourself. How to set them up is in discount codes for an event.

The ticket type name appears on the scanner when the QR is read, so at the door you see which group each child belongs to. If after publishing you want to open a new group, you can add a ticket type from the event dashboard.

What data to ask for (and from whom)

At a camp, the buyer is almost never the one attending. The ENTRÁ purchase form asks for the buyer's full name, email and ID number (DNI, the Argentine national ID), with phone optional: those are the adult's details. The child's details go in as the event's own questions:

  • The child's first and last name, and age (number question), both required.
  • A contact phone during the day and who is authorized to pick them up, if your camp needs it.
  • Allergies or anything the team needs to know, as free text.

Questions can be text, long text, number or a list of options, and you mark them as required or not. Two important things:

  • They are answered once per purchase, not per place. If someone signs up two siblings in the same purchase, they fill in the questions only once. Ask for one purchase per child, or set up a long text question for each child's details.
  • This is sensitive data. Ask only for what you will use and take care of the list you export. Whatever can wait, ask for by email from those registered before you start.

ENTRÁ doesn't validate ages: the age is declared by the adult. The ticket type ensures the right group, and you check the age yourself against the list.

At the door, every day

  • The same QR every morning. The scanner accepts it once per day and shows the buyer's name and the ticket type.
  • The list as an attendance sheet. Export it from the dashboard before the first day, with the answers to each question in their own column.
  • If someone arrives without the QR, they are looked up by the details of the adult who bought.
  • The reminder goes out once. In one QR for the whole event mode, ENTRÁ sends the "Es mañana" (it's tomorrow) email only before the first day. Anything about each day you announce yourself.

If a week fills up and you want to note down those left out, see sold out tickets and waiting lists.

Frequently asked questions

How do I load a summer camp that is sold by the week?

Load each week as a multi-day event, with its days from Monday to Friday and the one QR for the whole event option. That way each registered child comes in every day of that week with the same QR, once per day, and each week has its own cap.

Can I set a different cap for each day?

Not today. In ENTRÁ the cap is set per ticket type for the whole event, not per day. If each week or each day needs its own limit, load it as a separate event.

How do I separate kids by age?

With one ticket type per group, for example ages 4 to 6 and 7 to 9, each with its own cap. Add a required question with the age to confirm it. ENTRÁ doesn't validate age: the adult declares it and you check it.

If an adult signs up two siblings, how do I collect each one's details?

The event's own questions are answered once per purchase, not per place. The tidiest way is to ask for one purchase per child; if you prefer a single one, set up a long text question for each child's details.
